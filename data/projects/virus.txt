Project: Virus Screening (abstract)
----------------------------------

<strong>Abstract</strong> – Ever since the origin of human life, we have been infected by a wide range of viruses.  
These pathogens leave traces in our genome called <em>endogenous viral elements</em> (EVEs).  
Our study compares EVEs in Alzheimer’s‑disease (AD) patients versus cognitively‑healthy centenarians (CHCs).

Key findings
* AD genomes harbour <strong>more EVEs</strong> (p = 3.24 × 10⁻⁴, IRR = 1.27).  
* Specific chromosomal hotspots show higher viral integration in AD patients.

<strong>Why it matters</strong> – Viral infections over a lifetime may increase susceptibility to AD, highlighting the importance of infection‑prevention strategies.
