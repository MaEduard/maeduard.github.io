Project: Network Inference (scRNA‑seq)
-------------------------------------

Under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Ir. Reinders & Dr. Villegas Morcillo, I reproduced a network‑inference framework (Zamanighomi et al.) that extracts cell‑specific gene‑gene regulatory networks from single‑cell RNA‑seq data.

Deliverables
* Fully reproducible Python pipeline (available on GitHub).
* Detailed report describing methodology, validation, and results.
